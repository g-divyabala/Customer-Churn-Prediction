# Customer Churn Prediction – Business Recommendations

## Customer Retention Analysis

The analysis showed that customers with 0–12 months of tenure have the highest churn rate of 47.68%.

## Revenue Analysis

The 0–12 month customer group had 1,037 churned customers and approximately $68,954.25 in total monthly charges associated with those customers.

## Retention Offer Strategy

A hypothetical retention offer of $10 per month for three months was considered.

- Target customers: 1,037
- Offer cost per customer: $30
- Total offer cost: $31,110
- Estimated break-even customers: 156
- Break-even retention rate: 15.04%

## Final Business Recommendation

The company should prioritize new customers during their first year. It can use welcome discounts, regular follow-ups, better customer support and suitable pricing plans to reduce churn.

The retention offer should be tested with a small customer group before being introduced widely.
